# Code of Conduct — UAP-Video-Detector

## 🛸 Our Scientific Philosophy

The core objective of the **UAP-Video-Detector** is not to prove the existence of extraterrestrial life, but rather to apply the scientific method through computer engineering. Our premise is rooted entirely in **filtering and elimination**: we exhaustively isolate and remove variables and objects already thoroughly known to modern science—such as birds, commercial aircraft, drones, weather balloons, and satellites. 

Whatever remains after this rigorous filtering process becomes the true object of study: Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP). Our focus is to provide clean, reliable data completely free of everyday aerial noise.

---

## 🔬 Supporting Ufological Research

This project is explicitly engineered to serve as a robust tool for serious, data-driven ufological investigations and research. We recognize that modern ufology relies heavily on empirical, reproducible, and high-quality data. Therefore, we encourage researchers, data scientists, and aerial phenomenon enthusiasts to utilize this pipeline to validate sightings, catalog occurrences, and elevate the technical standard of open-source video analysis.

---

## 🤝 Community Guidelines

To maintain a highly productive, healthy, and engineering-focused development environment, all contributors are expected to follow these guidelines:

### 1. Scientific and Data-Driven Tone
* **Stay Focused on Data:** Discussions within Issues and Pull Requests must strictly revolve around algorithms, YOLO model enhancements, UI optimizations, and object-detection accuracy.
* **Avoid Sensationalism:** Conspiracy theories, purely speculative debates, or posts lacking technical evidence do not belong in the issue tracker. For broader conversations, community building, or sharing personal sightings, please use the **GitHub Discussions** tab exclusively.

### 2. Respect and Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration
* This repository bridges the gap between software engineers, academic researchers, and civilian ufologists. Please respect the diverse backgrounds and technical knowledge levels of every contributor.
* Constructive criticism regarding code design or pipeline architecture is highly encouraged, provided it is delivered in a cordial, professional manner focused entirely on improving the software.

### 3. Data Integrity
* When sharing video samples or datasets inside the `data/raw/` folder for community testing, ensure you do not upload digitally manipulated footage or known hoaxes. The integrity of our AI model depends entirely on the honesty and truthfulness of our input data.

---

## 🚨 Enforcement

Offensive comments, sensationalist spam, or repeated deviations from the project's technical scope will result in post moderation and, in extreme cases, a permanent block from the repository by the project maintainers.
